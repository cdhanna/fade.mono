# Left to Right
An object appears in the top-left + 1 (to avoid insta seamless victory), and must get to the right side before it hits the bottom. Any invalid swap causes the object to break and reset at the start.  

# Whale Krill
Invalid swaps don't give penalty, they turn the fish into krill. a krill combo scores for nothing. The goal is to get 50% of the board to krill, and then a whale comes and eats them all. 

# Lost Pair
Two marked fish need to be brought together. Invalid swaps reset the targeted fish.

# Sequence
Combos need to go off in a known order

# Shark
A shark moves everytime you do a swap; Any combo near it gets eaten

# Avoid Puffers
Don't pop any puffer fish for N moves

# Protected
Some fish are marked as protected; none of them can be involved in a swap until 25% of the round's points have been scored

# Sunbeam
A shaft of light moves one column every move; pop a combo in the lit column 3 times

# Continious Swap
Get 4 combos in a row where all your moves are valid swaps

# Seaweed
popping anything on the bottom row grows seaweed. Any pop directly above it causes the seaweed to grow up into that slot. The goal is to get a seaweed to the top. Anytime you swap seaweed out from its base, all of the seaweed above it breaks for zero points. Seaweed cannot be swapped up/down. 

# Wake
Every popped cell glows for about 6 moves. Connect the left edge to the right edge with an unbroken glowing path before the oldest part fades.

# Turtle
A turtle walks one cell to the right every move by itself, but only onto the kind of fish shown on its shell. Pave the road ahead of it so it reaches the right side.

# Cuttlefish
A cuttlefish cycles through the fish kinds in a known order, changing every move, and survives being popped. Pop it once as each kind.

# Constellation
Four marked fish of different kinds must be arranged into a shown shape (a line, a square, an L) using valid swaps.

# Tail
The cursor drags a tail through the last several cells it visited, and you may not cross your own tail. Make N combos without tangling. (maybe a game feature, not just a quest)

# Sleepers
Each move, one kind of fish is asleep, rotating in a known order. Groups of a sleeping kind don't pop until it wakes, and then they all go off at once. Bank 2 or more groups and set them off together.

# Echo
A ghost cursor repeats your swaps 4 moves later, at the same cells. If the ghost's swap is also valid when it happens, it counts. Get 3 echo combos.

# Pulse
A marked cell pulses every 4th move and turns its four neighbours into whatever kind is sitting on it. Get 3 combos out of pulses.

# Totem
A marked column must hold four specific kinds in a shown order. Any pop beneath it drops it out of place.

# Roll Call
Three specific fish are numbered 1, 2 and 3; each must be popped in a combo, in that order.

# Glass Fish
A fragile fish has to be lowered to the bottom row, but it shatters if it ever falls more than one cell at once.

# Reflection
A marked row must read the same from both ends.

# Tether
The cursor is roped to a buoy and can only move a few cells from it. Every combo moves the buoy to where the combo happened. Inch the buoy from one corner to the opposite one.

# Lantern
The cursor carries a light that loses fuel with every step and refills on every combo. Carry it to three beacons without it going out.

# Hatch
Three eggs sit on the board with countdowns of different lengths. Each must be part of a pop on the exact move its count reaches zero.

# Forecast
For this round you can see the next fish waiting above each column. Make 3 combos that are completed by a fish as it lands.

# Moorings
Three fixed cells each show a kind. Get a fish of the right kind onto all three at the same time.

# Trade Places
Two marked fish must end up in each other's starting cells.

# Pilot Fish
A small fish follows the path the cursor took, a few moves behind, advancing only on a valid swap. Lead it through three rings in order.

# Ferry
Starfish wait on the left edge. Pass over one to pick it up, carry it to the right edge, and repeat for three. An invalid swap drops the one being carried.

# Lasso
The cursor leaves a short trail. Close a loop around a marked fish before the trail fades, with at least one combo made along the way.

# Treasure
Something is buried under one cell. Every combo sends out a ping that shows whether it was closer or further than the last one. Pop on the right cell to dig it up.
